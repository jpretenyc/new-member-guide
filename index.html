Church Basics

Quick reference for meetings and the words you will hear at church.

Meetings

Every SundaySacrament meeting, then Sunday School and Relief Society or elders quorum. Children attend Primary; youth meet in their classes.
Fast SundayUsually the first Sunday of the month: fasting, fast offerings, and testimonies
Stake conferenceTwice a year, all the wards in the stake meet together in place of ward meetings
General conferenceFirst weekends of April and October, broadcast worldwide
Each yearWard conference; tithing declaration with the bishopric
AnytimeTemple worship, ward activities, institute, temple prep class

When Questions or Opposition Come

  • Expect opposition (2 Nephi 2:11). Have a plan: daily prayer and scripture study, the sacrament each week, and regular temple worship.
  • Pray, and study the scriptures and the words of living prophets.
  • Talk with your bishop, ministering brothers or sisters, or the missionaries.
  • Read Gospel Topics and the Gospel Topics Essays.
  • See how the Church works in the General Handbook.

Church Words

Ward.
Your local congregation. A smaller one is a branch.
Stake.
A group of wards, led by a stake president.
Bishop.
The volunteer leader of a ward, with two counselors (the bishopric).
Relief Society.
The organization for adult women.
Elders quorum.
The organization for adult men.
Primary.
Classes for children ages 3 to 11, with a nursery for younger children.
Calling.
A volunteer assignment to serve. Saying yes is one of the fastest ways to grow.
Sustaining.
Raising your hand to support someone called to serve.
Setting apart.
A blessing given when someone begins a calling.
Ministering.
Members assigned to care for specific people.
Covenant.
A sacred two-way promise with God.
Ordinance.
A sacred act performed by priesthood authority.
Temple recommend.
A card showing you are prepared to enter the temple.
Temple garments.
Sacred clothing worn by endowed members.
Proxy work.
Ordinances done for those who died without them.
Patriarch.
The stake officer who gives patriarchal blessings.
First Presidency.
The prophet and his two counselors.
Quorum of the Twelve.
Twelve apostles, special witnesses of Christ.
Fast offering.
A donation for the poor, at least the cost of two meals.
Elder / Sister.
Titles for missionaries. Members say “Brother” and “Sister.”
Seminary / Institute.
Religion classes for ages 14–18 and 18–30.
Latter-day Saints.
What members call themselves.
